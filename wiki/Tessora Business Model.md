# Tessora Business Model

Part of [[Tessora - Overview]]. See also [[Tessora Services]].

## Target Market

**Primary geography:** Philippines, starting with mid-to-large enterprises with heavy document sprawl.

## Candidate Starting Verticals

Pick one to launch with rather than going broad:

| Vertical | Why It Fits |
|----------|------------|
| BPO / HR-heavy organizations | Contracts, policies, compliance documents at scale |
| Real estate | Contracts, permits, property documents, compliance paperwork |
| Manufacturing / logistics | SOPs, supplier contracts, compliance & safety documentation |

*Which vertical to lead with should be chosen based on where pilot customer access or domain familiarity already exists.*

## Pricing Model

- **Seat-based pricing**, split by tier:
  - Executive seats (higher price point — dashboard/synthesis features)
  - Staff/operational seats (lower price point — retrieval and lifecycle features)
- Per-organization base fee (covering ingestion/setup) + per-seat monthly pricing
- **Premium tier:** on-prem / data-residency options for compliance-sensitive clients (HR, legal, finance-heavy orgs)

*Exact pricing to be modeled once a pilot customer or vertical is chosen.*

## Revenue Projections (Conceptual)

| Scenario | Seats | Monthly |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Small firm | 10 exec + 40 staff | ~₱25,000–40,000 |
| Mid-size enterprise | 20 exec + 200 staff | ~₱75,000–150,000 |
| Large enterprise | 50 exec + 500 staff | ~₱200,000–400,000 |
