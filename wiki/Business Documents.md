# Business Documents

Standardized document templates for [[Varix - Overview]] client engagements.

## Document Types

### Invoice
- Format: `VX-2026-###` (sequential numbering)
- Includes: scope, line items, payment terms, bank details

### Statement of Work (SOW)
- Scope definition, deliverables, timeline, milestones
- Change request process

### Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Mutual NDA with AI/data-handling clause
- Covers proprietary code, business data, and AI model outputs

### Retainer Agreement
Three tiers:
1. **Essential** — basic support + maintenance
2. **Growth** — active development + priority support
3. **Enterprise** — dedicated capacity + strategic consulting

### Client Intake Form
- Project requirements capture
- Technical stack preferences
- Budget and timeline expectations

### Project Handoff Checklist
- Code repository transfer
- Documentation package
- Deployment credentials
- Training materials
