# PokeCard PH — Business Model

Part of [[PokeCard PH - Overview]]. The billing mechanics live in [[PokeCard PH - Monetization]].

## Core Proposition

A two-sided marketplace connecting verified vendors with buyers through three transaction modes:

1. **Fixed-price sales** — Buy Now listings, graded or non-graded
2. **Trades** — card-for-card exchanges with in-app meetup coordination
3. **Auctions** — bidding on rare cards, sealed product, signed/merch items

The trust mechanism is **vendor-declared condition** — honest grading by the seller, not an AI score — optionally backed by a photometric 3D scan.

## What Each Side Gets

**Buyers:** cross-vendor browse/search, Buyer Protection Guarantee, in-app trade builder, interactive condition inspection, order tracking, payment via card/GCash/Maya/bank transfer.

**Vendors:** branded storefront with ratings, listing tools for all three modes, guided condition-scan tool, order/trade management dashboard, sales analytics, Shop Health scoring, tiered monthly billing.

## Revenue Streams

| Stream | Model | Activates |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Vendor monthly subscription | GMV-tiered flat fee + growth surcharge | After free trial |
| Featured/boosted listings | Flat fee for placement | Self-serve |
| Auction listing fee | Flat fee per auction | Per auction |
| Verified/authenticated listing fee | Paid scan certification badge | Per listing, opt-in |
| Protected trade fee | Flat fee for meetup verification | Per trade, opt-in |
| Buyer Pro subscription | Price alerts, early access, saved searches | Buyer-side, opt-in |
| Sponsored search placement | Vendors bid for ranking | Phase 2+ |
| Advertising | Adjacent product ads | Phase 2+ |
| BNPL referral cut | Facilitation fee via BNPL partner | Phase 3+ |
| Data/API access | PH card pricing data | Later, needs volume |

**Near-term priority:** subscription tiers → featured listings → verified-scan fee → auction fees → sponsored search.

## The 60-Day Free Trial

- Starts when shop is **approved** (post-verification), not signup
- ₱0 subscription with **full Premium-tier features** (deliberate — experiencing premium makes downgrading feel like a loss)
- Growth surcharge waived during trial
- GMV tracked silently; day-61 invoice reflects real trading tier
- 7-day and 1-day reminders with projected first bill
- Variant under consideration: "free until ₱5,000 GMV or 60 days, whichever comes first"

## Vendor Onboarding

**Identity & trust (blocking):**
- Valid government ID or DTI/SEC registration
- Phone verification via SMS OTP
- Linked payout account: GCash, Maya, or bank
- Agreement to Vendor Terms

**Shop setup:**
- Shop name, logo, banner, location, description
- At least one published listing before appearing in public search
