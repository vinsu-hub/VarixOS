# Voice & Positioning

Verbal identity for [[Varix - Overview]]. Companion to [[Brand Identity]].

## Voice Characteristics

- **Confident** — declarative statements, not hedging
- **Precise** — short service lines, no filler
- **Slightly clever** — wordplay that doesn't sacrifice clarity

## What We Are Not

- No AI-hype buzzwords ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive")
- No vague consulting-speak ("leverage synergies", "holistic approach")
- No unnecessary jargon that alienates non-technical clients

## Positioning Statement

Varix is the go-to technical partner for adaptable infrastructure in Southeast Asia. We design intelligent, adaptive systems that scale with your business.

## Service Lines (canonical)

Short, declarative, no fluff:
1. Web Development
2. AI Automation (n8n)
3. Mobile Development (iOS/Android)
4. AI Integration & RAG Systems
5. System Architecture
6. Custom & Embedded Systems

## Tagline

**"Engineered to Adapt."**

## Application

- Website hero: Confident one-liner + italic gold accent
- Client proposals: Direct scope statements, no padding
- Social media: Technical precision over marketing fluff
- Internal comms: Same voice — direct and actionable
