# Tessora Brand Identity

Part of [[Tessora - Overview]]. See also [[Tessora Voice & Positioning]].

## Name Origin

Tessora is built on the Latin root **tessera** — the individual tile used in Roman mosaics. A single tessera is just a fragment. Placed alongside others, it becomes a mosaic: a coherent picture more than the sum of its pieces.

That's the core idea: individual documents, contracts, reports, and records are fragments on their own. Tessora assembles them into a coherent, searchable, cross-referenced picture of how a company actually operates — without anyone needing to manually organize or tag a thing.

**Bonus layer:** a *tessera* also historically served as a token or identification credential — a nod to the platform's role-based access and security layer.

## Core Motif

The mosaic — fragments assembling into a coherent whole. Individual documents becoming a unified intelligence layer.

## Tagline

"From fragments to foresight."

Alt options:
- "The whole picture, from every document."

## Relationship to Varix Identity

Where [[Brand Identity|Varix's brand]] centers on the "X" as an intersection point (logic/creativity, human/machine), Tessora's identity centers on the mosaic — assembly, coherence, and the picture that emerges from fragments. Both share the engineering-first DNA but express it differently: Varix as the builder of adaptable systems, Tessora as the system that assembles coherence from scattered pieces.
