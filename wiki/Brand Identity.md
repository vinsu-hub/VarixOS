# Brand Identity

Core visual identity system for [[Varix - Overview]]. Canonical as of 2026-07-26.

## Core Motif

The "X" in Varix as a literal crossing/intersection point — of logic and creativity, human and machine, structure and flexibility.

## Palette

Near-black base + gold accent — the dark/gold CRT identity:

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| `--background` | `#050505` | Page background |
| Surface 1 | `#0a0a0a` | Cards, panels |
| Surface 2 | `#0f0f0f` | Raised elements |
| Accent gold | `#FFBF47` | CTAs, highlights, links |
| Borders | `rgba(255,191,71,0.12)` | Card borders, dividers |
| Text | `#F2F2F2` | Primary text |
| Muted | `rgba(242,242,242,0.6)` | Secondary text (6.63:1 on bg — WCAG AA) |

### Signature Effects
- CRT scanline overlay
- Hairline 1px-gap card grids
- Three.js glass-cube scene (hero)

### Superseded Direction
Original direction was deep navy/graphite + electric cyan (chosen 2026-07-22). The gold/CRT look from the variant build won the side-by-side decision.

Source: [[Varix - Overview]]

## Typography

| Role | Font | Style |
|------|------|-------|
| Display/headings | Cormorant Garamond | Light serif, italic gold accent words |
| Labels/nav/wordmark | JetBrains Mono | Uppercase, 8–12% letterspacing |
| Body copy | Inter | Clean humanist sans |

Hero example: "Engineered to *adapt.*" (italic gold on "adapt")

Source: [[Varix - Overview]]

## Applied Across Projects
