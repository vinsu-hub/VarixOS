# Tessora Mission & Vision

Part of [[Tessora - Overview]].

## Mission
To give Philippine companies a single, trustworthy source of truth across all their documents — so decisions at every level, from the frontline to the boardroom, are grounded in what the organization actually knows, not what's buried in someone's inbox or shared drive.

## Vision
To become the default document intelligence layer for mid-to-large Philippine enterprises — the system every new hire is trained on and every executive checks before making a call.

## How It Manifests

| Capability | Mission Alignment |
|-----------|------------------|
| Hybrid Search | Find any document by meaning, not just keywords |
| Grounded Q&A | Answers backed by actual company documents, not hallucinated |
| Role-Based Access | Right people see right documents, nothing more |
| Auto-Mapped Relationships | Surfaces hidden connections between documents |
| Local-First Architecture | Data stays on the company's own machines |
