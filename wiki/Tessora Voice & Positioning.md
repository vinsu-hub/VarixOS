# Tessora Voice & Positioning

Part of [[Tessora - Overview]]. See also [[Tessora Brand Identity]].

## Voice

Precise, grounded, trustworthy — like a compliance officer who actually writes well. Confident without being salesy. Specificity over superlatives.

**Good:** "Every query returns a source citation."
**Bad:** "We transform how you manage documents."

Avoid AI-hype buzzwords ("revolutionary," "game-changing," "transform your business"); let specificity and domain relevance do the selling.

## Positioning Statement

Tessora is a centralized, AI-searchable document system that gives executives cross-document decision support and gives operational teams fast, accurate, permission-safe document retrieval — built specifically for Philippine enterprises and localized for the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), English-Filipino-Taglish document mixing, and PH-hosted data residency.

## Competitive Framing

Not a replacement for Glean, Copilot, or Notion AI — a localized alternative for Philippine companies that need:

| Differentiator | Global Tools | Tessora |
|---------------|-------------|---------|
| Pricing | USD, enterprise-scale | PHP, sized for PH mid-market |
| Compliance | US/EU norms | Localized for RA 10173 / NPC |
| Language | English-first | English + Filipino + Taglish mixing |
| Data residency | Foreign-hosted | PH-hosted / on-prem options |
| User model | Flat, single-mode search | Two-tier: exec synthesis vs. operational retrieval |
