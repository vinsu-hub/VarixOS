# Kabiyahe — Overview

A Philippine-first user-generated travel platform built around **verified locals and location-based itineraries**, not hotels or airlines. Combines travel planning with social contributions — user-generated places, local verification, and the "Someone Been Here" (SBH) layer.

Part of [[Varix - Portfolio]].

## What It Is

A map-first platform where travelers search, plan, and save. Locals claim, curate, and earn. Every pin and itinerary is community-authored.

## Problem It Solves

The Philippines has 7,000+ islands but nearly identical travel content everywhere. Kabiyahe cuts through the noise with **verified local voices** — the Sari-sari Store is trustworthy; everything else is noise.

## Problem It Solves

Travel planning in the Philippines is fragmented across Facebook groups, blogs, and Google Maps. Kabiyahe centralizes real user-generated local knowledge.

## Core Pillars

- **Itinerary builder** — pre-made or DIY, each linked to the map
- **Social proof** — SBH numbers visible on every pin ("been here 1.5k")
- **Verified local layer** — rATM trust tier
- **Itinerary Gallery** — browse/save/link trips

## Broader Vision

Kabiyahe expands into the **Philippine local services marketplace** — local marketplace, help desk, API integration, transportation, events, eco-tourism. Same auth and same DB.

## Status

Planning phase. Not deployed.
