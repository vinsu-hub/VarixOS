# Kabiyahe — Strategy & Rollout

Part of [[Kabiyahe - Overview]].

## Strategic Insight

> "In our case ang ating content ay itineraries."

Incentivize locals to become verified contributors through **offered incentives** (local discounts, badges, "Sari-sari Store" recognition).

## Growth Strategy

Phase 1: Google Ads SEM + Organic. Phase 2: Add Facebook/IG as you expand beyond search intent.

## Sari-sari Store

The "Sari-sari Store" is a **trust badge and contributor role** for verified locals who curate real, ground-level knowledge.

## Search

**`search_path = 'public, extensions'`** — lets PostgREST resolve `to_tsvector(...)` against `public` table columns without schema prefix. Used with Supabase RPCs.
