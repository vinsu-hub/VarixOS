# Voice & Positioning

Part of [[Varix - Overview]]. See also [[Brand Identity]].

## Voice
Confident, precise, slightly clever — like a senior engineer who's also a good writer.

Avoid AI-hype buzzwords ("revolutionary," "game-changing"); let specificity do the selling.
- Good: "we cut manual ticket routing by 80%"
- Bad: "we transform your business"

## Positioning Statement
"Varix builds the adaptable infrastructure — web, AI automation, and mobile — that lets growing businesses run on logic, not guesswork."
