## Mobile App Development (iOS & Android)
Native and cross-platform mobile apps built with performance and usability at the core — from customer-facing consumer products (see [[Featured Work|Farmsy]]) to internal operational tools.

## AI Integration & RAG Systems
Beyond simple chatbots — we build retrieval-augmented systems that let your AI tools actually understand your business data, documents, and processes, so responses are accurate and grounded, not guessed. Three tiers of capability:
1. **Hybrid-Search RAG with Citation Verification** — dense vector search + BM25 keyword search + cross-encoder reranking, plus a verification pass that checks every citation actually supports its claim. Eliminates unsourced answers.
2. **Multi-Modal Document RAG** — OCR/LLM extraction over messy real-world documents (scanned PDFs, tables, inconsistent formatting), with a validation layer and confidence-gated human review queue.
3. **Agentic RAG** — a retrieval router that decides what to fetch and when, reformulates weak queries, and retries thin results rather than letting the system hallucinate.

## Custom & Embedded Systems
Beyond web and mobile — low-level embedded systems (Arduino firmware, sensor integration, hardware state machines) to fully automated software bots for platforms like Discord and Slack. If a business process can be modeled, it can likely be automated, regardless of layer.
