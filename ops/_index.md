# Ops

Recurring operational documents — metrics, schedules, open items, weekly reports.

## What lives here
- `metrics.md` — pipeline value, open tasks, portfolio snapshot
- `schedule.md` — daily/weekly schedule
- `headlines.md` — latest updates and logs
- `varix-open-items.md` — tracked open items across Varix projects

## What doesn't live here
- Project-specific plans (use `projects/<domain>/`)
- Finished deliverables (use `content/`)
- Raw research (use `wiki/`)

## Navigation
Check `_index.md` before searching this folder manually.
