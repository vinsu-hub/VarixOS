## Farmsy — Local Farm Discovery Platform (Netherlands & Belgium)
Full end-to-end product build: web platform plus native iOS and Android apps. Connects users with 8,000+ verified farms across the Netherlands and Belgium — farm shops, organic producers, pick-your-own locations — with map-based discovery, SKAL-certified organic listings, and direct farm contact details. Subscription model (yearly and lifetime tiers), live on Google Play, iOS in progress.

Demonstrates: full-stack web architecture, cross-platform mobile development, geolocation/map-based product design, Stripe subscription integration, production deployment at real user scale.

## Specialized RAG Research Deployment — Multidimensional Poverty Intervention System
See [[MPI RAG System - Overview]] for the full system breakdown.

A Retrieval-Augmented Generative AI System for Multidimensional Poverty Intervention across Health, Education, and Living Standards, developed and deployed for barangays in Los Baños, Laguna.

Combines local government CBMS (Community-Based Monitoring System) data with a RAG pipeline — open-weight LLM inference, dense embedding retrieval, vector database — to identify and prioritize poverty-intervention needs at the barangay level. Synthesized real demographic data spanning 37,000+ households and 109,000+ individuals across 14 barangays.

Demonstrates: applied RAG architecture in a real civic/government-data context, end-to-end deployment (including live field deployment via secure tunneling for a public defense presentation) — the same retrieval-and-grounding discipline used in commercial document-intelligence work.
